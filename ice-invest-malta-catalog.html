Private Yacht Club.
Three Members Only.

An exclusive club of three — each owning one-third of a world-class explorer yacht, sharing nothing but the costs. What you do with your time aboard is entirely your own affair.

The Concept

Three individuals jointly acquire a single explorer yacht under 500 GRT, each holding exactly one-third of the vessel — covering one-third of the purchase price and annual operating costs. Every number is open for inspection. Full transparency, open book — no soft costs, no hidden markups.

Each member receives 12 weeks of exclusive use per year. What you do with those weeks is entirely your decision: enjoy the yacht privately, place it on charter, or swap weeks with a fellow member. No restrictions, no obligations. Your time is yours.

Charter at €350,000 per week — revenues flow directly to the member who owns that week. A single charter week covers a meaningful share of your annual operating costs.
